How to delete your Bingo Arena data

Replace [PRIVACY EMAIL] with your own address before you publish. Facebook and Google ask for this page when you set up sign-in.

Delete it yourself (fastest)

  1. Open Bingo Arena and log in. This works for accounts created with email, Google or Facebook.
  2. Tap your name at the top to open your Profile.
  3. Under Your data, press Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm.

Your account, coins, items, progress, crew membership and scores are deleted straight away. You can first press Download my data to keep a copy.

Cannot log in?

Email [PRIVACY EMAIL] from the address you signed up with, or tell us the display name and the Google or Facebook account you used. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

If you signed in with Facebook or Google

Removing Bingo Arena in your Facebook or Google settings stops the sign-in link, but it does not delete your game data. Please use one of the options above to delete the data as well.
