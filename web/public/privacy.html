3. What other people can see

Other players can see your display name, level, title, avatar frame, rank and scores on leaderboards, in matches, in events and when you share a challenge. Nothing else about you is public. A challenge link shows your display name and score to whoever opens it. If you share a result picture, you decide where it goes.
